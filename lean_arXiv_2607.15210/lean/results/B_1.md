# Proposition B.1: Entropy of the one-channel output body after antisymmetric postprocessing

Complete.

The actual antisymmetric postprocessed matrix-body entropy image equals the explicit shuffle image. Its attained minimum has the exact leading coefficient and O(k^-5/2) error for every fixed p>0. Actual CP/TP, a unitary spectral decomposition, the normalized feasible body, all Taylor bounds, sharp quadratic optimization, and minimum attainment are proved.
