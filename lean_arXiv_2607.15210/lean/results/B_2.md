# Proposition B.2: Entropy of the limiting Bell output after antisymmetric postprocessing

Complete.

The actual tensor-postprocessed isotropic Bell matrix has exactly the weighted dm/bellNu entropy formula. Its Kraus representation, antisymmetric partial-trace recurrence, surjectivity, complete eigenvalue multiplicities, matrix normalization, and spectral regrouping are proved. The actual matrix entropy has O(k^-3) error for every fixed r and p>0; the unprocessed r=1 estimate improves to O(k^-4).
