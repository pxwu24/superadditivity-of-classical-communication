# Theorem V.1: Single-output and Bell-output asymptotics after antisymmetric postprocessing

Complete.

Both actual fixed-k entropy limits and both actual large-k entropy expansions are assembled for the genuine CP/TP antisymmetric postprocessed channels. Fixed-k existence holds for every k>=r with k^2*t>1. The actual body minimum and actual postprocessed Bell matrix have the stated coefficients, with stronger O(k^-5/2) and O(k^-3) errors respectively, for all fixed p>0 including p=1. The new HaarSequence endpoint supplies the proved convergence theorem for arbitrary admissible rank sequences.

## Assumptions and dependencies

- Fixed integer r>=1,t in (0,1),p>0; for each k>=r with k^2*t>1 take n->infinity first, then k->infinity.
- In the final Lean wrappers, parameter r represents the paper particle count r+1, covering every positive integer particle count. The weighted Bell spectrum is used only in the eventually valid range k>=2(r+1).
- The block-modified convergence theorem and the Bernoulli free-sum law are proved. No external convergence premise is required by the new paper endpoint.

Paper dependencies: III.1, IV.1, V.2, B.1, B.2.
