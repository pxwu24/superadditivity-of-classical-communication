# Proposition V.2: Eigenvalue shuffling

Complete.

For every actual Hermitian input matrix, an explicit basis of the actual alternating tensor subspace diagonalizes antisymmetric postprocessing with exactly the subset-sum eigenvalues AppendixB.shuffle. The signed permutation projector, Slater basis, completeness, change of basis, channel coefficient, and dimension binomial(k,r) are all proved. The fixed Slater-coordinate matrix map is proved completely positive and trace preserving, and is explicitly conjugate to the restricted tensor operator.
