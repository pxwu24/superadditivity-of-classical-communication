# Lemma C.1: Reduction of the entropy minimum

Complete.

Every minimizer on the actual normalized feasible body has one strictly higher coordinate and equal remaining coordinates. The attained minimum equals h_k(L*) and gives the lower bound for every certified L. Compact minimization, boundary exclusion (including the quartic case), Lagrange multipliers, two-value reduction, and the explicit feasible negative-curvature perturbation are proved under the general paper hypotheses.
